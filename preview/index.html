FAQ

Questions churches ask.

What counts as a finished Short?

A Short counts when you export the final 1080p video. Uploading sermons, reviewing moments, drafts and previews never count.

Does it work with audio-only sermons?

Yes. Bring in .mp3, .wav or .m4a files and use the Audio Message template.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 11 computer. Transcription and video work happen on your computer.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
