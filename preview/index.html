    How does the free trial work?

    14 days, up to 10 finished Shorts, no card. Then choose a plan to keep exporting.

    What counts as a finished Short?

    Only the final 1080p export. Uploads, drafts and previews never count.

    Does it work with audio-only sermons?

    Yes. Use .mp3, .wav or .m4a with the Audio Message template.

    What do I need to run it?

    A Windows 11 computer.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. You keep access until the paid period ends.
