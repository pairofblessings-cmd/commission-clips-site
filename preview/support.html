Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Email us: commissionclipssupport@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing and what you saw. In the app, Settings → View safe diagnostics gives details you can copy into your email; it never includes your sermons or transcripts.

Your plan

  • Upgrade: choose a plan on the Plans page. After checkout you get a license key.
  • Activate: in Commission Clips, open Settings (bottom-left) → Your plan, paste the key and press Activate.
  • New computer: a paid license works on up to three computers. To move it, remove it on the old computer first (Settings → Your plan).
  • Cancel or change billing: use the link in your purchase receipt email. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refund: email us within 14 days of your first Cornerstone purchase for a full refund. See the Terms.

Common questions

  • What counts toward my plan? Only exporting a finished Short. Uploading sermons, reviewing moments, drafts and previews never count.
  • When does my allowance reset? At the start of each allowance month. The Plans page in the app shows the exact date.
  • Does it work with audio-only sermons? Yes — .mp3, .wav and .m4a, with the Audio Message template.
  • What do I need? A Windows 11 computer. Transcription and video work happen on your computer.

Privacy

Your sermons, transcripts and videos stay on your computer. See the Privacy Policy, including how to delete your data.
