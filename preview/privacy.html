Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Commission Clips is a desktop app that turns sermons into short videos on your own computer. Your sermons, transcripts and videos stay on your computer unless you choose to post a video to a social account you connected. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you.

Who we are

Commission Clips is owned and operated by Nicholas Josiah Tassiello, an individual in Oklahoma, USA ("we", "us"). Contact: commissionclipssupport@gmail.com.

What stays on your computer

Sermon files, transcripts, projects, Shorts and exports are processed and stored only on your computer. We have no account system, no telemetry, no analytics tracking and no crash reporting. Error logs are kept locally and are never uploaded automatically.

Payments

When you buy Cornerstone, checkout happens on our store provider's secure page (Lemon Squeezy). They process your payment, taxes and receipt under their own privacy policy. We never see or store your card details. We receive your order details, such as your name, email, plan and license key, so we can support your purchase.

Connected social accounts

  • What we access: your account's name and picture, permission to upload the videos you choose, and your posts' statistics. We never post without your action, and we do not read your messages, followers' personal data or other people's content.
  • Where it is stored: access tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI / Credential Manager). Statistics are cached on your computer for display.
  • Sign-in helper: some platforms (TikTok and Meta) require a private app key during sign-in, which cannot be kept inside a desktop app. A small sign-in helper we run exchanges the one-time sign-in code for your token and passes it straight back to your computer. It does not store tokens, videos or personal data.
  • No sharing or selling: we do not sell, rent or share data from your accounts, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Google and YouTube data

Commission Clips uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Commission Clips' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove Commission Clips' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect an account in Commission Clips (Social → Disconnect): the app deletes that account's token and cached statistics from your computer and asks the platform to revoke access.
  • You can also revoke access in the platform's own settings (Google account permissions, TikTok "Manage app permissions", Facebook/Instagram "Apps and websites").
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folders removes everything it stored.
  • Because we keep no copies on our servers, there is nothing else to delete. Questions or requests: commissionclipssupport@gmail.com.

Children

Commission Clips is intended for churches and ministries and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
